Why the Status page shows cost now and quality later

The tap sees a recall the moment it happens — how many claims, how many tokens. Whether any of it helped can only be judged once the session is over and distilled, so a recall made this morning has no verdict until tonight. Pulse keeps those two clocks apart rather than inventing a per-turn score.
